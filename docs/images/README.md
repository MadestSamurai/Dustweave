## Support artwork

`support-dustweave-zh.png` and `support-dustweave-en.png` are localized documentation graphics, not application screenshots. Created on 2026-10-19, they use original programmatic knot artwork and print-inspired typography. The Chinese and English READMEs each reference their matching image.

The author's supplied Ko-fi, WeChat Pay and Alipay QR images were decoded locally and re-encoded unchanged with error correction Q and four-module quiet zones. The codes use a high-contrast ink color, subtly rounded modules and finder corners, without center overlays or generative editing. Each QR was decoded again from both saved compositions at widths of 1800, 1040, 800 and 640 pixels; all 24 comparisons matched the source payloads. This verifies QR contents and readability, not a payment transaction.

支持图片以作者提供的三个二维码为输入，程序化重绘与排版，未改变收款链接。中英文分别使用独立图片；编织图形为原创线条绘制，二维码保留完整扫码留白。两张成品在四种尺寸下均通过解码比对。
