- Interruptions distinguish connection, identity, permissions, components, game dialogs and unconfirmed results, with a suggested next step. Current connection health is separate from the most recent operation.
- Export a diagnostic ZIP of the last 7 days to a chosen location. The dialog includes **WeChat: SuJakads0133** and **QQ: 1104563414**, with copy support. Credentials are filtered and nothing is uploaded automatically; share logs privately with the developer.
- Latest Release and [Quark download folder](https://pan.quark.cn/s/0ee553c60c0d?pwd=5AgC) links appear above update history. Access code: **5AgC**.
- Browse plans, history and separate task selections for each account; return to account tasks after a batch. Includes first-time plugin preparation and settings-save improvements.

**If automatic updating fails, you may need to download the full package manually.** Close the old app and extract into a new folder without mixing versions. Accounts and settings remain in their user-data directory.
