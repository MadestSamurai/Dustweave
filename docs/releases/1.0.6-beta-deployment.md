## Changes

Setup now closes safely after confirming Skip; cancellation leaves it open. Its persistent language selector applies Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or English globally without resetting progress or an edited account label. The integrated Territory tool validates the live hosted session instead of demanding a standalone connection receipt. Layout also recovers from an already-reported failed start.

Enabling that route requires a trusted source entry and extending the release workflow's explicit asset allowlist and validation.
