## Acceptance workflow correction

An initial test incorrectly applied first-migration entry ordering to a direct
upgrade between two protocol-2 native entries. That assertion was corrected;
first migration remains covered by its separate bridge/inventory acceptance.
Further runs exposed a test-window teardown race after successful restoration.
Manual computer-use intervention was stopped and is no longer a release step.

Acceptance now separates durable update/startup assertions from cleanup. Only
exact fixture-owned processes are disposed after assertions, without focus,
coordinates or tray actions. Disposal is logged and does not claim to test normal
user shutdown. Scripted native acceptance passed without computer use; all 22
existing account-vault files were byte-identical before and after. Six saved
startup receipts and the out-of-fixture rejection guard also passed lifecycle
checks without launching another application. Failed/aborted runs are retained.
