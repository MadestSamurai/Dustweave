## Delivered behavior

Launch-and-run is inside the right side of the daily summary card. The batch page no longer opens a second sandbox window for task selection. Once workers have finished or stopped, returning dismisses the overview and shows account tasks in the existing window. Saved overviews are not reopened automatically.

Account progress remains available by switching the account selector. Completed tasks show their outcomes and completion times and are unchecked by default; manual selections survive switching accounts. Game reset expires yesterday's presentation and resets drafts, including the case where a new cycle arrives before the UI observes the expired old record. Worker results remain presentation data, not host-side resumable transaction journals.

The release also includes native continuous Mirror battles, simplified weekly collection settings, signed-in connection recovery and seven-day compact diagnostic retention.
