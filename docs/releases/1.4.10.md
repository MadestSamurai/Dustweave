# Dustweave 1.4.10

- **快速赶路（实验性）**：在日常设置的全体设置中开启。日常步行前按需使用飞奔；已有加速、技能冷却、不可用或药量不足时继续普通移动，不等待效果结束。默认关闭，遵守天赋药保留下限，不在收割或交互中打断操作。
- **自定义料理**：仅收藏购买模式增加料理选择弹窗。左侧搜索料理及材料，右侧维护烹饪顺序；支持批量添加、移除和上下调整。购买收藏商品后，按顺序尽量制作，后一道使用更新后的库存；缺料、未解锁或容量不足时跳过，不追加非收藏采购，不出售料理。
- **内置小游戏**：SECRET VISION、连连看源码迁入 `src/SecretVision` 与 `src/Sichuan`，使用织尘的主题、语言、加载动画与运行互斥。先展示窗口，再后台连接；有效旧设置自动沿用，日志纳入主程序导出及清理。独立仓库归档，后续维护集中到 Dustweave。
- **切换效率**：日常小游戏直接在当前进程中准备统一组件，减少额外辅助进程和固定等待。仍核验账号、控制权、请求及组件就绪状态；不提前准备无关工具。

- **Fast travel (experimental):** opt in under overall daily settings. Uses the movement-speed talent before walking when needed and available; an active effect, cooldown or insufficient pills does not add a wait. Off by default and respects the pill reserve. Harvesting and interactions are not interrupted.
- **Custom cooking:** favorites-only trading offers searchable recipe and ordered cooking lists, with multi-selection and reorder controls. Cook from the refreshed inventory in priority order after purchases. Skip unavailable recipes, do not buy additional ingredients, and keep all cooked dishes.
- **Integrated minigames:** SECRET VISION and Sichuan now live under `src/`, sharing styling, language, window-first loading and automation ownership. Valid old preferences are retained; diagnostics join the host's export and cleanup. The standalone repositories are archived.
- **Faster switching:** weekly minigames prepare the shared component in the daily process, avoiding an extra helper launch and fixed wait. Identity, ownership and readiness checks remain in place; unrelated tools are not eagerly prepared.

Portable 自带运行库；Lite 需要 .NET Desktop Runtime 8 x64。使用 GitHub Release 完整 ZIP 或软件内 OTA；更新失败时请手动下载完整包并解压。
