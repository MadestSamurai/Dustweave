# Dustweave 1.5.17

此版本用于从旧版本自动升级至 1.6.0。确认重启后，会继续下载并安装包含修复的正式版，账号和设置保持不变。

更新中断时，重新打开织尘即可继续。首次安装或需要重新下载的用户，请直接下载 1.6.0。

This transition upgrades existing installations to 1.6.0. After the restart, the final update downloads and installs automatically, preserving accounts and settings. Reopen Dustweave to resume an interrupted update. For a fresh installation or manual recovery, download 1.6.0 directly.
