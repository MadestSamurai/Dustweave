## Scope and validation

Scoped evidence reads inherit the remaining deadline of the owning operation
instead of abandoning long battle or travel waits after three seconds. Freshness
requirements remain unchanged. A genuine observation timeout holds the current
queue step, preserves submitted operations and confirmed weekly progress, and
does not attempt the next task or navigate out of an unfinished operation.
Nested waits and concurrent account scopes have separate regression coverage.

Sandbox discovery now checks actual box registration before accepting an
inherited global file-root setting. A new account without a sandbox can create
one instead of being incorrectly sent to binding repair.

The reported game-loading delay is not attributed to manual settings transfer:
the user did not invoke it, and that transfer runs only after an explicit choice.
The available long-frame records do not identify a network, storage, rendering
or sandbox-resource root cause. This release addresses premature interruption,
not a demonstrated reduction of the game's loading time.

Full synthetic validation passed **7,042 checks in 130 groups**. The initial
packaging wrapper reached its eight-minute limit shortly before the runner
finished successfully. The completed report and recorded source inputs were
reused by the canonical packaging script. A subsequent missing runtime
translation was fixed in `src/Desktop/Localization/runtime.json`; the package
records this artifact recheck and reran packaged language/UI validation.
Application logic did not change after the successful full run. Both final
editions passed startup, language, tool/session and shared-component checks.
Build CI and the release workflow also passed.

The real released **1.5.7** self-copy updater passed **10 cases** across Lite and
Portable: upgrade, failed-target startup rollback, interrupted replacement
recovery through the host and preserved helper, and refusal of a damaged
baseline without installation writes. Exact installed bytes, target startup and
account/plugin sentinels were checked. Interruption was injected, not a physical
power-cut test. No game was launched, connected to or operated in this acceptance.
