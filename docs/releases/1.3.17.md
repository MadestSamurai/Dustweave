# Dustweave 1.3.17

## 中文

- 修复空间管理只统计部分日志的问题：完整显示数据与缓存目录占用，包括组件缓存、商品表、更新文件及迁移后的缓存。
- 清理过时的可重建缓存和已结束的旧更新备份；保留账号、设置、当前组件及未完成的恢复记录。自动清理在空闲时运行。
- 在“软件设置 → 日志与缓存”迁移缓存到其他磁盘。复制并校验全部文件后才切换位置，失败或取消保留原缓存；账号和更新恢复目录保持原位置。
- 数据或缓存所在磁盘可用空间不足 1 GB 时显示提醒，提供完整目录占用、清理和迁移入口。可勾选“今日忽略”，按用户本机时间下次早上 8 点恢复提醒；重启软件后仍有效。未勾选时同一次低空间状态最多每小时提醒一次。
- 正常空间检查不扫描目录，只有需要提醒或打开空间管理时才后台统计完整占用；不会因为提醒而中断日常。
- 若 OTA 失败，请手动下载完整包并解压到新目录，保留 `app` 文件夹；账号与设置继续沿用。

Portable 自带运行库；Lite 需要 .NET 8 Desktop Runtime（x64）。1.3.7–1.3.16 支持直接差分更新，更早版本请手动下载。本次变更不涉及游戏操作策略。

## English

- Storage management now counts the complete data folder and relocated cache, including component builds, trade catalogs and update files, instead of reporting only selected logs.
- Cleanup removes obsolete rebuildable caches and completed old update backups while preserving accounts, settings, recent components and unfinished recovery state. Automatic maintenance runs while idle.
- Move rebuildable cache to another drive from **Software settings → Logs & cache**. Files are copied and verified before switching locations; cancellation or failure preserves the previous cache. Accounts and update recovery remain in their original locations.
- When a data/cache drive has less than 1 GB available, a reminder shows its free space, complete folder usage and cleanup/migration actions. **Ignore today** survives restarts and expires at the next 08:00 on the user's local clock. Without it, a continuing low-space condition is shown at most hourly.
- Routine free-space checks do not scan folders. Complete usage is measured in the background only when a reminder or storage management needs it; reminders do not interrupt automation.
- If OTA fails, extract the full package into a new folder and keep the `app` folder. Existing accounts and settings are retained.

Portable includes the runtime; Lite requires .NET 8 Desktop Runtime (x64). Versions 1.3.7–1.3.16 support direct differential updates; earlier versions require manual installation. Gameplay strategies are unchanged.
