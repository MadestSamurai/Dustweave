## Delivered behavior

Diagnostics now supports a manual cleanup preview and idle automatic log retention. The default is 7 days with a 512 MB soft target; the latest 48 hours, account/settings stores, task/retry progress, unresolved operations, plugins and exported archives remain protected. Busy, changed and unsafe filesystem entries are skipped. No actual user logs were deleted during acceptance.

Trading jointly allocates current inventory, future shop supplies and expected drops from known weekly routes. Forecast resources are never executable inventory. The planner corrects independently overvalued future ingredients, indefinitely postponed profitable cooking and repeated deferral of raw sales. Actual inventory, quotes, funding and unresolved operations remain execution boundaries.

With fixed current game rules, the 365-day mean-path processing/trading gain rose from 233,094,549 to 238,224,623 (+2.20%). Three paired random paths improved 2.18%–2.24%. These results exclude the raw value of free drops. Average-drop forecasting alone slightly underperformed the no-drop ablation on the mean path; the report records that limitation and the remaining arrival-date/high-price-date approximation. This is not proof of an attainable global annual optimum.
