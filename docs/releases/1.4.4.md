# Dustweave 1.4.4

## 中文

- **活动战斗结算修复**：自动战斗最后一场已经结束时，正确识别活动专用返回按钮，避免在胜利画面等待 15 分钟并影响后续日常。恢复时先核对本轮结算，不重复提交已消耗的战斗。
- **救赎之塔固定次数扫荡**：新增可选模式，忽略成长加成是否已满或石头是否已足够，按设定次数扫荡。仍遵守每日钥匙保留下限、可扫荡关卡和活动状态限制，不使用付费次数；默认保持原来的按需扫荡。
- **保留血之鸡尾酒邮件**：新增默认关闭的选项。开启后，包含血之鸡尾酒的整封邮件都会保留，包括混合附件；周日也不自动领取，由用户自行安排。活动流程补领邮件同样遵守此设置。
- **周收集恢复**：修复遇到天赋提示动画或战斗技能演出时中断的问题，待当前演出结束后继续处理对应提示。

## English

- **Event battle completion**: recognize the event-specific return button after the last automatic battle, avoiding a 15-minute wait on the victory screen that can block subsequent tasks. Recovery reconciles the current result without replaying consumed battles.
- **Fixed-count Salvation sweeps**: an optional mode sweeps a specified number of times even when growth is maxed or sufficient stones are already available. Daily-key reserves, sweep eligibility and event-state checks still apply. Paid attempts are never used; growth-based sweeping remains the default.
- **Keep Blood Cocktail mail**: a new option, off by default, retains the entire message whenever its attachments include Blood Cocktails, including mixed attachments. It stays enabled on Sundays for manual collection and also applies to activity-related mailbox collection.
- **Weekly collection recovery**: handle talent notices and skill animations without interrupting collection; wait for the current animation before handling the relevant notice.
