## Scope and acceptance

Trading no longer treats an ingredient's per-stack limit as its total inventory
capacity. Purchases and cooking share the native normal-inventory slot budget,
including remaining space in existing partial stacks. Executable orders use
integer slot requirements and do not borrow slots from later cooking or selling.
Future planning retains a fractional slot forecast, reserves unrelated food slots
and initial fragmentation, and never directly executes forecast-only orders.
Custom cooking also supports multiple stacks of one dish. Existing spending caps,
gold reserves, protected ingredients and processing preferences remain effective.

The dismantling reward handoff now waits for the preceding preview to finish its
closing animation within the existing timeout. It does not click through unrelated
dialogs, resubmit dismantling, or weaken identity and stop checks.

Focused validation passed **561 checks in eight groups**. Full synthetic validation
passed **7,457 checks in 132 groups**. The first packaging wrapper reached its
eight-minute deadline; its child test process subsequently completed successfully
and recorded every group. The completed results and source-input identity were
retained and reused in final packaging, without repeating the full suite. The
failed wrapper evidence remains separate from the final passed package checks.

Both final editions passed startup, resources, integrated-tool and native-binding
checks. The binding update adds only the generated normal-inventory property and
its usage; existing curated bindings are retained. Recorded inventory replays
tested several free-slot scenarios. The old capture lacked a complete normal-bag
slot count, so those scenarios do not establish one exact retrospective purchase
total for the affected account. No live game was started, connected to or operated.

The actual released **1.5.9** self-copy updater passed **10 cases** across both
editions: upgrade, failed-target startup rollback, interrupted replacement recovery
through the host and preserved helper, and damaged-base refusal without installation
writes. Exact files and account/plugin sentinels were verified. Interruption was
injected; this was not a physical power-cut test.
