## Delivered changes

MANSION RUNAWAY's 80-chain goal retries early when remaining candy cannot reach the target or repeated route probes show poor prospects. Future native reward candies are included in the bound. Reaching 80 preserves the round until native settlement confirms the result. Entry recovery distinguishes missing context from an unavailable activity. Hosted tools retain their selected account's process during multiple-instance use and never reconnect to another account when that process exits.

Before publication, one live trial abandoned three unpromising maps, reached 80 on the fourth round in about 96 seconds, and completed settlement in about 128 seconds. Random maps and enemies mean this is evidence for that trial, not a per-round guarantee.
