# Dustweave 1.4.1

## 中文

本版将潘多拉挑战模式的实验成果接入内置工具，并修复连接间歇中断和旧账号无法删除的问题。

### 潘多拉大逃脱

- 新增角色选择，默认沿用游戏当前选择。角色列表来自游戏，未解锁角色不可选；更换角色在下一局生效，不中断当前对局。
- 挑战模式按推荐武器、辅助道具及配对可行性选择升级和换牌。确认剩余栏位与换牌机会无法凑齐至少五组配对时才重开。刷货币模式不使用这套挑战配装要求。
- 移动决策考虑怪物速度、攻击、输出能力、回血道具与短时风险，优化 BOSS 冲刺预警、跳跃落点和墙边退路。
- 15 分钟后逐步转向生存优先。眩晕不会降低对怪群的安全要求；临近结束时更重视利用眩晕拖延时间。
- 后期被困墙角时，没有已确认安全的脱困路线就保持站定。只有当前可确认的连续眩晕保护足以覆盖脱困路线时才移动，不把尚未拾取的下一件道具视为已获得保护。
- 移除旧的“满装备后站定”选项，由新的场景判断统一处理；保留不自动选择装备的选项。
- 窗口支持滚动、紧凑尺寸和三种语言，角色选择在切换语言或模式时保留。

**验证范围：**这套策略已在一次实机挑战中坚持到 20 分钟。单次通过不代表所有角色、养成和随机局都能通关；整体胜率仍需更多样本。发布验收另行覆盖离线回归、真实发行包及更新器，不修改用户账号或游戏数据。

### 连接与账号

- 修复本机连接在完成一次请求后、下一次接收准备前短暂消失的问题，减少运行中的偶发读取失败。
- 暂时读不到新身份信息时不再直接误报账号已变化。潘多拉等待新数据后继续，期间不发送操作；确认账号或游戏进程变化时仍停止。
- 无法解密的旧账号可在账号管理中删除。操作需要二次确认，并在删除前重新核对记录，避免误删刚修复或更新的凭据；不会改动游戏当前登录状态。

## English

This release integrates the tested Pandora challenge strategy into the built-in tool and fixes intermittent local connections and deletion of unreadable saved accounts.

### Pandora Escape

- Select an unlocked character from the game's list, or keep the current game selection. A change takes effect next round without interrupting the current run.
- Challenge equipment planning considers recommended weapons, support items and feasible pairings. Restart only when the remaining slots and replacements cannot produce at least five pairs. Currency farming keeps its own equipment rules.
- Movement considers enemy speed, attacks, damage output, healing and short-term risk. Boss dash warnings, jump landing areas and escape space near walls receive updated handling.
- Survival takes priority after 15 minutes. Stun effects do not make dense enemy groups count as safe; delaying danger becomes more valuable near the time limit.
- When cornered late in a run, hold position unless a verified escape route exists. Movement requires enough currently confirmed stun protection for that route; uncollected pickups do not count as protection.
- Remove the old max-equipment standing option in favor of these contextual decisions. Manual equipment selection remains available.
- The tool supports compact scrolling layouts and three languages, preserving character selection across language and mode changes.

**Validation:** one live challenge trial reached 20 minutes. This does not establish a success rate across characters, account upgrades or random runs. Release validation separately covers offline regressions, actual packages and updater recovery without changing user accounts or game data.

### Connections and accounts

- Keep the local connection endpoint available between requests to prevent intermittent read failures.
- Distinguish temporarily missing identity observations from an actual account change. Pandora waits for fresh data without issuing commands, then resumes; real account or process changes still stop execution.
- Delete unreadable saved accounts after confirmation, without signing in again. Recheck the record immediately before deletion so newly repaired credentials are preserved. The game's current login is not changed.
