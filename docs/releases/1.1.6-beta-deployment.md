## Delivered behavior

Trade settings prepare their own read connection and evidence paths, without requiring another daily task first. Missing initial purchase records use the game's fresh-stock rules. Free-draw previews recover from unsubmitted stale previews while rechecking free counts. Weekly collection waits for the field interface to recover, then reacquires the current exit with bounded recovery and account/process/map guards.

The opt-in break-even resale strategy uses spare funds after the profit plan. It selects only extra goods whose discounted buy price equals their 120% sale price, excluding bargaining fees from that selection. Actual potion costs remain in the ledger. The setting is off by default, saved per account, translated into three languages and used by automatic trading as well as the planning panel.

Diagnostic exports include standalone trade reads and field-route recovery state. Compact logging preserves the actual write sequence when timestamps tie; legacy ambiguous timestamp groups retain their full evidence.
