# Dustweave 1.4.19 legacy migration delivery

Released on 2026-10-27 (Asia/Hong_Kong) from `maintenance/1.4-migration`, based on v1.4.18. This retirement notice does not backport 1.5 changes. GitHub latest remains **v1.5.4**.

## Startup behavior

- Source/tag: `b7c441fbdec3dba89987044d8d4e6f0f50277828`.
- The Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English notice says 1.5 was released more than 48 hours ago, explains the retired 1.4 support status and links to the current complete downloads.
- Closing the notice exits the requesting app. Exact Chinese or English acknowledgment allows only the current owner lifetime; authorized children inherit consent, and a fresh launch asks again.
- Ordinary startup, scheduled/parallel/selected execution, connection helpers and tools are blocked until acknowledgment. Offline self-tests and updater recovery remain available.
- During an actual update, the notice child retains the native transaction nonce and validates its owner. Rendering the notice acknowledges healthy startup without granting automation consent. Native recovery checks and the single-instance/control locks remain intact.
