# Dustweave 1.4.5

## 中文

- **断线后接续原队列**：重新连接或重启游戏后，核对账号、角色及重置周期，从当前游戏进度恢复。修复旧记录长期停在待恢复状态、无法继续的问题。
- **周收集路线修复**：统一导航与任务执行对受保护地图的判断，避免在血之狂想等卡带的地图间反复往返。增加无进展检测与精简路线记录，手动停止后也可以导出诊断。
- **今日吸收地图**：时间线与结果详情可以查看当天实际完成吸收的卡带、地图和时间；NPC 任务途中顺路吸收会单独注明。普通启动与隔离执行合并记录，较旧或空记录不覆盖已完成结果。
- **减少不必要的隐身**：只在实际步行进入有敌人或风险未知的路段时使用并复用已有加成。从战斗地图传送点直接前往安全区、进入地图准备压制，以及确认没有敌人的路段不额外使用隐身。
- **简化任务设置**：不再接取仅能在狩猎场完成的周 NPC 任务；好感度咨询统一使用普通对话；移除没有设置内容的展开按钮。
- **内置工具统一维护**：装备助手、使徒防守和无限抽抽乐迁入织尘主项目。统一加载窗口、连接互斥与界面主题，并更新工具指南、原仓库维护说明及支持入口。

## English

- **Resume after disconnection**: after reconnecting or restarting the game, verify the account, character and reset period, then recover from current game progress. Old interrupted records no longer prevent resuming indefinitely.
- **Weekly route recovery**: navigation and execution now agree on maps protected by NPC quests, preventing repeated transfers between maps such as those in Blood Rhapsody. Bounded progress checks and compact route diagnostics are available even after a manual stop.
- **Today's absorption details**: view confirmed cartridge/map names and times from the timeline and result details. Absorption performed along an NPC quest route is identified explicitly. Ordinary and isolated runs merge completed results without older or empty records erasing them.
- **Less unnecessary stealth**: use and renew stealth for actual walking segments with enemies or unknown risk. Direct waypoint travel to a safe area, entering a map for suppression, and confirmed clear routes do not trigger extra casts.
- **Simpler settings**: stop accepting hunting-ground-only weekly NPC quests, use normal friendship conversations, and remove empty settings expanders.
- **Unified tool maintenance**: Equipment Assistant, Apostle Defense and Infinite Gacha now live in the main repository, with shared loading, connection exclusion and themes. Tool guides, repository maintenance notices and support links are updated.
