# Dustweave · 织尘 1.1.10-beta

## 中文

- MANSION RUNAWAY 的 80 连锁模式优先连续吃豆。剩余糖果不足，或多次规划仍找不到有希望的路线时，会提前重开，不再耗完整局；判断包含后续连锁奖励糖果。
- 达到 80 连后保留本局，等待游戏结算确认再停止。普通模式继续使用综合策略。
- 改善小游戏续局时入口信息丢失的恢复，区分入口丢失与活动不可用，避免覆盖其他活动。
- 修复多开时内置工具误选其他游戏进程的问题；重新连接保持当前账号，目标游戏退出后不会转连另一个账号。

实机验证中，连续放弃 3 张不理想的地图后，第 4 局达到 80 连：整轮约 96 秒达标、128 秒完成结算。地图与追兵存在随机性，这不是每轮保证。

## English

- MANSION RUNAWAY's 80-chain mode prioritizes uninterrupted candy collection. It retries early when too few candies remain or repeated searches find no promising route, allowing for future native chain rewards.
- Once 80 is reached, the round is preserved until the game confirms the result. Normal play retains its balanced strategy.
- Improves recovery when the minigame entry is lost between rounds. Missing entry data and an unavailable event are distinguished without replacing another activity.
- Fixes integrated tools selecting the wrong game process with multiple instances. Reconnection stays with the selected account and never switches to another when its game exits.

In one live trial, the tool restarted three unpromising maps and reached 80 on the fourth round: about 96 seconds to reach the goal and 128 seconds to finish settlement. Random maps and pursuers mean these timings are not guaranteed.

**If automatic updating fails, download the full package manually.** Close the old app and extract into a new folder without mixing versions. Accounts and settings remain in their user-data directory.

| Package | Runtime | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Portable** | Includes the .NET 8 desktop runtime | First-time users or systems without the runtime |
| **Lite** | Requires .NET 8 Desktop Runtime x64 | Systems with the runtime; smaller download |
