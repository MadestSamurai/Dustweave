# Dustweave · 织尘 1.1.1-beta

## 中文

- 诊断页新增日志与空间管理：先统计可释放空间，再手动清理；也可在空闲时自动清理。默认保留7天、容量目标512 MB，始终保护最近48小时。
- 账号、设置、任务与补跑进度、待核对操作、插件及导出的日志包不参与清理。正在运行或被占用的记录会跳过。
- 跑商与料理改为共同分配材料，综合当前库存、后续商店供货和已知周收集路线的掉落期望，优先总收益。未来预计物品不会当作当前库存下单。
- 修复有收益的料理被长期推迟，以及给不同材料重复计算未来用途导致的过量保留。受其他原料限制后剩余的材料，可以用于其他有收益的料理。
- 每次交易仍以实际库存、报价和资金重新核对；料理等到120%报价确认后出售。

**如果自动更新失败，可能需要手动下载完整软件包。** 关闭旧版，将完整包解压到新目录后运行；不要混放不同版本。账号和设置保留在原用户数据目录。

| 分发包 | 运行环境 | 适合 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Portable** | 包含 .NET 8 桌面运行时 | 首次使用或未安装运行时 |
| **Lite** | 需 .NET 8 Desktop Runtime x64 | 已安装运行时，下载更小 |

## English

- Adds log retention and storage management in Diagnostics: preview reclaimable space, clean manually, or enable idle automatic cleanup. Defaults to 7 days and a 512 MB target; the latest 48 hours always remain.
- Accounts, settings, task/retry progress, unresolved operations, plugins and exported archives are preserved. Running or busy records are skipped.
- Trading and cooking jointly allocate current inventory, future shop supplies and expected drops from known weekly routes to maximize total profit. Forecast items never become executable inventory.
- Fixes profitable cooking being postponed indefinitely and excess retention caused by independently overvaluing shared ingredients. Surplus ingredients can fund other profitable recipes when a preferred recipe lacks its complementary materials.
- Execution continues to check actual inventory, quotes and funding; prepared food is sold only after a confirmed 120% quote.

**If automatic updating fails, download the full package manually.** Close the old app and extract into a new folder without mixing versions. Accounts and settings remain in their user-data directory.

| Package | Runtime | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Portable** | Includes the .NET 8 desktop runtime | First-time users or systems without the runtime |
| **Lite** | Requires .NET 8 Desktop Runtime x64 | Systems with the runtime; smaller download |
