## Live validation boundary

Before packaging, authorized live checks entered Evil Castle, dismissed its already-issued season reward, walked to the gate, entered the Tower lobby and skipped because no difficulty was cleared. Event checks inspected normal and challenge progression, found the highest fully cleared sweep target, and exited because free attempts were exhausted. No paid attempts or tower keys were consumed.

Native consecutive-battle wins/defeat, fallback sweep settlement and Tower sweep debit/reward settlement still need live validation on an eligible account. Synthetic checks do not establish those live results. This limitation is included in the public release notes.
