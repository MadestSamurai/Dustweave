# Dustweave 1.3.9

## 中文

- 优化内置使徒防守的格位查找与属性评分，减少重复计算及临时内存分配。
- 加入离线退火得到的阶段重开规则，综合盘面输出、金币、积怪与培养情况，优先缩短连续尝试的累计通关时间。没有“缺少7／8等就必须退出”的门槛。
- 保留现有召唤、升级、出售与换位决策。关闭自动续局或选择本局结束后停止时，不主动重开；仅刷最高等使徒目标不受影响。
- 原独立验证集的预计首次通关耗时减少约26%。这是正常条件下的离线模拟估计，不保证实机耗时或单局胜率；新规则会放弃部分原本可能通关的局。

若 OTA 更新失败，请手动下载完整包并解压到新目录，保留 `app` 文件夹。账号与设置继续沿用。

## English

- Optimizes board lookups and attribute scoring in the built-in Apostle Defense planner, reducing repeated calculations and temporary allocations.
- Adds a stage-based restart rule selected through offline simulated annealing, weighing damage, gold, enemy backlog and development to reduce cumulative time to a clear. Lacking tier 7/8 units does not automatically end a match.
- Retains summoning, upgrading, selling and positioning decisions. Turning off automatic continuation or stopping after the current round prevents deliberate restarts; the rare-summon-only goal is unchanged.
- The original independent validation estimated approximately 26% less time to the first clear under normal simulated conditions. This is not a real-game timing or per-attempt win-rate guarantee: some potentially winning attempts are deliberately abandoned.

If OTA fails, extract the complete package into a new folder and retain the `app` folder. Existing accounts and settings are preserved.
