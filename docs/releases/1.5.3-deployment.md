## Correction

The reward-row binding check introduced in 1.5.2 assumed every native control
derived from `LoopScrollItemBase` belonged to an initialized `LoopScrollRect`.
Fixed restaurant banners, manually routed rows and independently owned talent
controls do not share that contract. The additional check now applies only to
the tactical-training mission popup. Other controls retain the established
native active/interactable checks. The mission list still excludes templates,
pooled/loading rows and foreign owners and waits for its deferred refresh.
Its native value-type mission data is compared by value rather than boxed
reference identity.

Restaurant selection recognizes numbered settlement banners, including the
full-income `Content3` layout. Child preparation tools inherit the host's pinned
plugin selection. Reward-cleanup messages distinguish an open reward display
from a destination that is not ready or has not refreshed. Missing or ambiguous
button errors now have explanations.
