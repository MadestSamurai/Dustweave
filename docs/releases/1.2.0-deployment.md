## Delivered changes

Task presets can be saved on Today and assigned per account. Optional run-on-open uses the selected account queue. Closing offers exit or minimize to tray. Daily settings follow the viewed account. Pass switching refreshes mission controls, weekly routes retain blocked locations while continuing other available cartridges, and trading supports partially unlocked shops.

Trading reserves allow limited near-term proceeds from existing unlocked inventory already allocated for premium sale: 50% of receipts within seven days, capped at 25% of usable cash. Actual purchases remain limited by the current balance, reserve and spending limit. Forecast drops and new purchases do not enlarge that allowance.
